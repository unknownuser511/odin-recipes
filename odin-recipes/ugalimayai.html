UGALI MAYAI RECIPE

my second dish

description

the dish contains ugali and mayai etc.

ingredients

  • 1 cup of maize flour
  • 2 cups of water
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup of milk
  • 1/4 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 tablespoon of butter

steps

  1. Mix the maize flour and water to form a thick paste.
  2. Boil the paste until it is cooked through.
  3. Beat the eggs and mix with the milk and salt.
  4. Melt the butter and pour it over the egg mixture.
  5. Serve the ugali and mayai together.
